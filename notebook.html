project 01 · UX / UI

FlowState

you earn your break by finishing the task, not by waiting out a timer.

the final design ✦
~80%of students procrastinate regularly

Phone blockers run on a timer that ends whether or not you worked, and to-do apps do nothing when you reach for your phone. Nothing checked that the task was done.

the brief

role
solo: research, UX, UI, testing
module
User Interfaces, Imperial · March 2026
tools
Figma, paper prototypes, think-aloud tests
3

the journey

  1. 1talk to people4 interviews with grad students: starting is the hard part
  2. 2map the momentone flow: tempted → paused → break it down → prove it → reward
  3. 3sticky-note itpaper prototypes; 3 buttons became 2
  4. 4wireframegreyscale lo-fi to lock the hierarchy
  5. 5make it realhi-fi in Figma: coral = action, purple = focus, teal = success
  6. 6test it6 think-aloud sessions, then iterate
"my problem mainly is starting…"
— participant 3
4

the happy path

Marcus opens Instagram out of habit. FlowState steps in:

"Instagram is paused", not blockedbreak it down into 15-min bitesprove it: photo or friend's code15:00 of guilt-free scrolling!

"Now I can know what I need to do next. That makes it far easier."
"I felt good not opening Instagram because it reminded me not to."
5

what testing changed

  • only the app icon triggers it → the notification does too (people tapped that first!)
  • tap the tiny upload button → the whole preview is tappable
  • "photo" + "file upload" → one upload proof option

what I'd do next time

Test with people beyond postgrads, since procrastination covers chores and working life too, and design the missing flows: onboarding, calendar sync and how strict the app should be.

project 02 · IoT

Smart Plant Buddy

"is my green thumb not so green?"

meet my Polyscias 🌱
£19of parts, vs £129 for a smart pot
693readings in a week, 100% uploaded

the brief

role
solo: hardware, firmware, cloud, analysis
module
Sensing & IoT, Imperial · Dec 2025
stack
ESP32, Firebase, Chart.js, Python
7

the journey

  1. 1calibratesoil: dry air vs. submerged. <1500 = thirsty
  2. 2wire it upESP32 + soil, light, temp & humidity sensors
  3. 3send it to the cloudFirebase every 15 min, live via WebSockets
  4. 4give it a facea rule-based mood shown on a tiny OLED
  5. 5live with ita week of data, a dashboard, a pilot with family
it's happy! (^ ^)

moods: happy (^ ^) · thirsty (O O) · hot (> <) · drowning (@ @) · ok (:—)

8

what the data said

soil ↔ temp r = 0.74warm days dry it faster
  • ✓ 237 ms uploads (goal <500)
  • ✓ 95% mood accuracy
  • ✓ 99.7% uptime
  • ✓ caught all 7 waterings, 0 false alarms
  • ✓ 80% of testers checked the face over the numbers
9

things that went wrong

  • jittery soil readings (WiFi interference) → average 10 samples
  • green gunk on the probe (copper oxide!) → clean it or swap in a spare
  • laggy charts → downsample 700 points to 200

next time

Automatic watering, a camera to check leaf health, more species profiles, and proper security: auth rules, no hard-coded WiFi and certificate pinning.
